About this build

Live scoreboards: Browser fetches from publicly accessible ESPN endpoints. This is an unofficial integration, may change or be blocked, and is not affiliated with ESPN.

Research: Selected game summary and box-score statistics when provided by ESPN.

Math: American odds, expected value, boosted payouts, independent-leg parlays, and bet settlement.

Not yet implemented: Independent player projections, verified sportsbook odds feeds, historical backtesting, injury-adjusted models, correlated parlay simulations, cross-device synchronization, and alerts. Those require further data/model infrastructure.

Privacy: Tracker data stays on your device, not a central server. Do not put passwords or API secrets into this public website.

Sources: ESPN for public scoreboard data; calculations performed locally.

EdgeLab is an analysis tool, not financial advice or a guarantee of profit. Only wager where legal and within your limits.
