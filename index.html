How rankings work

For matching outcomes across sportsbooks, EdgeLab compares the best available price to a consensus probability estimated from other bookmakers' two-sided markets after proportional vig removal. It ranks by estimated expected return, with an uncertainty buffer based on disagreement across bookmakers.

This is a market-consensus heuristic, not an independently trained prediction model. Sportsbook odds are correlated and can be stale. A positive estimate is not proof of an edge. Games with fewer than three distinct bookmakers or incomplete opposing sides are withheld from ranking. No automatic wagers are placed.

What the model can and cannot do

Historical hit rates describe past games, not the probability of the next game. The experimental probability below uses a simple Beta(1,1) smoothing prior and a 90% approximate interval. It does not adjust for opponent, injury, role changes, weather or market movement. Samples smaller than five are insufficient for a useful estimate. Do not treat this as a validated betting edge.

College player logs can be especially incomplete. ESPN endpoints are unofficial and can fail or change format; unavailable categories are not filled with fabricated values. Saved inputs remain on this device only.
