How rankings work

For matching outcomes across sportsbooks, EdgeLab compares the best available price to a consensus probability estimated from other bookmakers' two-sided markets after proportional vig removal. It ranks by estimated expected return, with an uncertainty buffer based on disagreement across bookmakers.

This is a market-consensus heuristic, not an independently trained prediction model. Sportsbook odds are correlated and can be stale. A positive estimate is not proof of an edge. Games with fewer than three distinct bookmakers or incomplete opposing sides are withheld from ranking. No automatic wagers are placed.
